Frequently Asked Questions

- ## What APIs does KLIPY provide?







  - [GIF API](https://docs.klipy.com/gif-api): Incorporate our collection of the newest and most popular GIFs straight into your application. Adding a dynamic and captivating search experience to your messaging or content offerings can boost user engagement.
  - [Sticker API](https://docs.klipy.com/stickers-api): Let your users express themselves with popular stickers. Our powerful search tool makes it easy for them to find and share the perfect sticker in seconds.
  - [Clip API](https://docs.klipy.com/clips-api): Integrate our database of your famous movies and video clips to create a more memorable user experience. Make it possible for consumers to find and share memorable moments from popular media.
  - [Meme API](https://docs.klipy.com/memes-api): Give users access to our collection of the most well-known and relatable memes on the internet to keep your content interesting, up-to-date and highly shareable.
  - [Emoji API](https://docs.klipy.com/ai-emoji-api): Add AI-powered emoji generation to your platform. This API includes built-in tools for search, trending topics, and categories, making it easy to create and find the perfect emoji.
  - [Ads API](https://docs.klipy.com/advertisements): Monetize your application from day one with our optional Ads API. You have complete control to enable or disable advertisements at any time.

- ## How to use KLIPY API?







1. Get a Test Key: Leverage a Test API Key from the [Partner Panel](https://partner.klipy.com/api-keys), allowing up to 100 API calls per hour for building and testing your integration before requesting a production key.
2. Integrate KLIPY: Utilize our comprehensive [API Documentation](https://docs.klipy.com/) to guide you through the integration process. The documentation offers clear instructions and code examples to help you seamlessly incorporate KLIPY’s content into your app or platform. If you have any questions, feel free to reach out to us at **developers@klipy.com**.
3. Display KLIPY Branding: Please follow our [attribution guidelines](https://drive.google.com/drive/u/3/folders/1ix5_5221kgbJHPqhCxwPsqHlHexhQP2w) and implement all the required branding into your integration to maintain consistency and ensure transparency for end-users.
4. Customize Content Filters: In the [Partner Panel](https://partner.klipy.com/manage-content), set content filters to control the type of GIFs, Clips, Stickers and Memes displayed and block specific keywords to suit your platform’s audience.
5. Request Production Access: Once your integration is fully tested, request production access by filling out a simple form in the [Partner Panel](https://partner.klipy.com/manage-content) to unlock unlimited API calls.
6. Implement Monetization (Optional):  Integrate seamless, user-friendly Ads into your platform via our [Ads API](https://docs.klipy.com/advertisements). By displaying these Ads in place of any GIF, Clip, Sticker or Meme in Trending or Search results, you can generate substantial revenue while enhancing the user experience.

- ## How to migrate from Tenor to KLIPY?







1. Get a Test Key: Leverage a Test API Key from the [Partner Panel](https://partner.klipy.com/api-keys), allowing up to 100 API calls per hour for building and testing your integration before requesting a production key.
2. Swap Endpoint: Simply replace the base URL tenor.googleapis.com with api.klipy.com in your codebase, plug in your [KLIPY API Key](https://partner.klipy.com/api-keys), and your GIF experience continues to work.

3. Attribution: Update it according to [our guidelines](https://partner.klipy.com/manage-content).
4. Production Access:  Submit a request through the [Partner Panel](https://partner.klipy.com/api-keys).

- ## Where can I find full API documentation?







You can visit [docs.klipy.com](http://docs.klipy.com/) to view our comprehensive and simple [API documentation](http://docs.klipy.com/), which includes examples and instructions.

- ## Are there usage limits or rate limits on the API?







Yes, the restrictions vary according to the type of API key you're using:



  - Test Key: For full development and testing, the maximum number of API calls is 100 per hour.

  - Production Key:  Provides limitless requests to guarantee your live application's scalability and seamless operation.

- ## Can I filter content in KLIPY API?







Yes, you have the ability to set content filters in the [Partner Panel](https://partner.klipy.com/manage-content) to control the type of GIFs, Clips, Stickers and Memes displayed and block specific keywords to suit your platform’s audience. For more details how filtering works visit our [API Docs](https://docs.klipy.com/content-filtering) .

- ## Is there an example integration of KLIPY API?







You can review our [IOS](https://github.com/KLIPY-com/klipy-ios-demo-app) and [Android](https://github.com/KLIPY-com/klipy-android-demo-app) Demo App Source Code to explore how KLIPY integrates with modern apps - with or without Ads. It’s built to help you launch fast and scale smoothly with clean UX and minimal latency.

- ## Is the Ads API mandatory for using KLIPY?







No, the Ads API is completely optional. It is a monetization tool you can choose to use if you want to start earning revenue from your app immediately.

- ## How can I control the display of advertisements?







Manage Ads for any [API Key](https://partner.klipy.com/api-keys) by selecting Update App Details  from the three-dot menu. Use the toggle to turn on or off advertisements for that specific app.

- ## Where should I display advertisements?







Advertisements should be displayed in the Trending, Search, Categories, and Recents sections of all types of our content. To maximize your chances of receiving ads and increasing revenue, ensure you provide all required parameters in the API requests, along with as many recommended parameters as possible. You can find the full list of parameters in the "Advertisement" section of our API documentation at [https://docs.klipy.com](https://docs.klipy.com/).

- ## Who are KLIPY’s ad partners?







KLIPY is maximizing the quality and revenue of ads displayed through partnerships with top DSPs and SSPs internationally. Check the list of our ad partners on [Developers page](https://klipy.com/developers).

- ## What happens if I lose my API key?







Your API keys are always securely saved, so don't worry. By signing in to your account in the partner panel, you can locate your keys whenever you want.





If you have trouble logging into your account, please email  support@klipy.com  to reach the support team.

- ## How can I report a bug or suggest a feature?







We value your feedback and are always looking to improve! If you find a bug or have a feature
suggestion, you can reach out to us in a few different ways:



  - Contact Form:  Click the chat icon in the bottom right corner of the KLIPY website and fill out the form to describe your case.
  - Support Email:  Send a detailed message to our support team at support@klipy.com, and we'll get back to you soon.
  - Reddit:  Join our [community](https://www.reddit.com/r/klipycom/) to ask questions, showcase your project, and get feedback from the KLIPY Team and fellow developers.
  - Discord:  Join our [server](https://discord.com/invite/jhmNHFRzQY) to report issues, suggest features, and interact directly with the KLIPY community.

- ## Which KLIPY domains should be allowlisted?







If your organization uses a firewall, proxy, secure web gateway, parental controls, or restricts outbound traffic, your IT team may need to allow list KLIPY’s API and media delivery domains so content and thumbnails load correctly.





View the full list of required domains in our Network Requirements and Firewall Allow list guide
[here](https://docs.klipy.com/network-requirements).
